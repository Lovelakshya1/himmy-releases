**Himmy Anime** is a sleek, modern, and ad-free anime streaming and direct-download client for Android. Built with Jetpack Compose, it features seamless AniList integration, high-speed dual-server downloads, a cinematic edge-to-edge player, and deep visual customization.

---

## ✨ Features at a Glance

### ⚡ Dual-Engine Direct Downloads
- **Multi-Server Provider Support:** Download episodes directly from high-speed stream servers (**Eenie** & **Meenie**).
- **Quality & Size Badges:** Choose your preferred video resolution (**1080p Full HD**, **720p HD**, **480p SD**, or **360p**) with accurate file size estimates before starting.
- **Direct Video Container:** Files are packaged into standard `.mkv` and `.mp4` formats ready for offline viewing in any media player.

### 🚀 Persistent Background Download Engine
- **App-Wide Background Service:** Downloads continue uninterrupted even when navigating away from the watch page, browsing menus, or minimizing the app.
- **Global Floating Download Capsule:** Monitor live progress, speed, and cancel/resume downloads from anywhere in the app with a sleek floating mini bar.
- **Android Notifications:** Real-time download progress bar right in your device's notification tray.

### 🎬 Cinematic Edge-to-Edge Player
- **True Immersive Fullscreen:** Enjoy 100% borderless, edge-to-edge video playback with zero UI bars or cropped edges.
- **Seamless Minimizing:** Minimize back to the watch page without reloading the video or interrupting audio playback.
- **Sub & Dub Switching:** Toggle effortlessly between original Japanese audio with subtitles and English dubs.
- **Episode Auto-Tracker:** Automatically remembers your exact timestamps and progress for effortless resuming.

### 🌸 Native AniList Synchronization
- **One-Click OAuth Login:** Connect your AniList account securely.
- **Live Progress Sync:** Automatically updates episode watch counts, ratings, and statuses directly to your profile.
- **Custom Lists & Favorites:** Access your Planning, Watching, Completed lists, and favorite anime right from your dashboard.

### 🎨 MySpace & Deep Personalization
- **Dynamic Color Themes:** Personalize the app with custom color palettes (Rose Quartz, Cyberpunk Neon, Sunset Orange, Deep Violet, and more).
- **Custom Typography:** Switch between handpicked font families to match your style.
- **Custom Profile Assets:** Customize your avatar, profile banners, and background wallpapers.

### 🔄 Built-in In-App Self Updater
- **Automated Update Checker:** Check for new versions with a single tap inside the MySpace tab.
- **Live Changelog Viewer:** Read what's new and improved before updating.
- **Direct In-App Upgrade:** Downloads the latest APK with a live speed meter and launches the Android package installer directly—no external browser links required.
- **Zero-Waste Storage Auto-Purge:** Installer files are automatically deleted after updating, keeping your phone's storage clean.

## 📥 How to Install

1. Navigate to the **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** section of this repository.
2. Download the latest **`app-release.apk`** file.
3. Open the downloaded file on your Android device.
4. If prompted by Android, enable **"Allow from this source"** in your device settings to permit installation.
5. Tap **Install** and enjoy streaming!

> **Requirements:** Android 7.0 (Nougat, API Level 24) or higher.

---

## 🔄 How to Update

You can keep Himmy Anime up to date in two ways:

1. **Inside the App (Recommended):**
   - Open **Himmy Anime** $\rightarrow$ Go to the **MySpace** tab.
   - Scroll down to the **App Updates** section and tap **Check Updates**.
   - Tap **Download & Update** to install the new version automatically.
2. **From GitHub Releases:**
   - Download the newest `.apk` from the **[Releases](../../releases)** tab and install it over your existing app (all your watch history and settings will be preserved).

## 🔒 Permissions Explained

| Permission | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| `INTERNET` / `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Streaming video, fetching anime metadata, and syncing with AniList. |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` / `DATA_SYNC` | Keeping episode downloads active when switching apps or locking the screen. |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Showing live download progress in the Android notification drawer. |
| `REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES` | Allowing the in-app updater to prompt the native Android package installer. |
