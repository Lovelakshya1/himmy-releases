## Features

### 🎬 Watch
- **Custom ExoPlayer Engine:** Powered by AndroidX Media3 with native HLS streaming and direct hardware track control.
- **Dynamic Video Quality:** Select 1080p Full HD, 720p HD, 360p, or Auto adaptive bitrate directly from the active stream.
- **Dynamic Subtitle Switching:** Only actual server-provided subtitle tracks are displayed, with true hardware-level text overlay switching.
- **Subtitle Styling Studio:** Customize your subtitle typeface (Inter, Cinzel, Playfair, Oswald, JetBrains Mono), font size, text colors (White, Yellow, Cyan, Green), outlines, and drop shadows.
- **Multi-Server Streaming:** High-speed streaming pipelines with automatic failover (Miney, Eenie, Meenie / Anixo Zoko, MegaPlay, Flixcloud, Ani.pm).
- **Sub & Dub Support:** Effortlessly toggle between Japanese audio with subtitles and English dubs.
- **Quality of Life:** Auto-skip opening/ending themes, playback speed adjustment (0.50x – 2.00x), and automatic episode timestamp bookmarking.

### 📥 Download & Offline Hub
- **Dedicated Downloads Hub:** Manage storage usage, view downloaded episode sizes, and play saved episodes offline.
- **Isolated Offline Cinema Player:** Plays downloaded `.mp4` and `.mkv` files with accompanying subtitles in a dedicated top-level fullscreen window with sensor auto-rotation and hidden navigation bars.
- **Progressive Stream Scanner:** Zero-wait download sheet that queries servers in parallel and immediately reveals download qualities as each server answers.
- **Background Downloads & Mini Capsule:** Continue downloading in the background while switching apps or with the screen locked, accompanied by a floating progress capsule and download speed meter.
- **Resilient HLS Downloader:** Downloads and stitches `.ts` segments into standard video containers with embedded `.vtt` subtitles.

### 📖 Read
- **Weeb Central Primary Provider:** Reverse-engineered direct provider delivering authentic, uncompressed manga panels with zero `leech.jpg` placeholder redirects.
- **Multi-Provider Fallbacks:** Instant fallback access to MangaPill, MangaKatana, and MangaDex.
- **Versatile Reading Modes:** Continuous vertical Webtoon scroll, Left-to-Right, and traditional Japanese Right-to-Left paging.
- **GPU Color Shaders:** Read comfortably anytime with custom shaders including Sepia, Invert Dark Mode, Night Warmth, and Monochrome.
- **Chapter Drawer:** Quick navigation drawer for jumping between chapters and scanlator releases.

### 📡 Stay Up to Date
- **Airing Radar & Broadcast Schedule:** Weekly day-by-day broadcast calendar in your local timezone with live countdown timers.
- **Anime News Magazine:** Real-time news curated from Crunchyroll, Anime News Network (ANN), MyAnimeList (MAL), and Anime Corner.
- **Anime Theme Music (Openings & Endings):** High-fidelity music playback with synchronized video and a floating background mini-player.

### ☁️ Sync
- **One-Tap AniList Integration:** Fast, secure OAuth token login.
- **Two-Way Progress Sync:** Automatically updates episode progress, scores, and watch lists.
- **Custom Lists:** Full access to Planning, Watching, Completed, Paused, and Dropped lists directly from your dashboard.

### 🎨 Customize (MySpace)
- **Curated Theme Palettes:** Teal, Rose Quartz, Cyberpunk Neon, Sunset Orange, Deep Violet, and more.
- **Custom Typography:** Outfit, JetBrains Mono, Inter, Poppins, Comfortaa, Fredoka, and Amatic SC.
- **Avatar & Profile Studio:** Hundreds of anime character presets, custom banners, wallpapers, and background tinting.

## Installation

1. Go to the **[Latest Release](https://github.com/Lovelakshya1/himmy-releases/releases/latest)**.
2. Download **`app-release.apk`**.
3. Open the file on your Android device.
4. If prompted, enable **"Allow from this source"** in device settings.
5. Tap **Install** and enjoy streaming!

> **System Requirements:** Android 7.0 (API Level 24) or higher.

## How to Update

- **In the App (Recommended):** MySpace $\rightarrow$ Check Updates $\rightarrow$ Download & Install.
- **From GitHub:** Download the newest APK from [Releases](https://github.com/Lovelakshya1/himmy-releases/releases) and install it over your existing build (all your watch history, bookmarks, and settings are preserved).

## Permissions Overview

| Permission | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| `INTERNET` / `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Streaming video & music, downloading manga panels, metadata, and AniList synchronization. |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` / `DATA_SYNC` | Keeping background episode downloads active when switching apps or locking the screen. |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Showing live download progress meters and media playback controls in the notification shade. |
| `REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES` | Prompting the Android package installer for direct in-app upgrades. |
