## Why Himmy?

- **A UI you will actually enjoy.** Material 3, a live mesh-gradient home, and a bento-style discovery grid.
- **Make it yours.** Color palettes, custom fonts, hundreds of avatars and banners, and profile backgrounds.
- **Anime and manga in one app.** Stream, download, and read without switching apps.
- **No ads. Ever.**

---

## Features

**Watch**
- Edge-to-edge video player with two streaming servers and automatic failover
- Sub and dub switching, subtitle and audio track selection, skip intro/outro
- Continue watching, with your timestamp saved automatically

**Download**
- Background downloads with pause and resume, even with the screen locked
- Choose 1080p, 720p, 480p, or 360p, with file size shown
- Standard `.mp4` / `.mkv` files that work in any player

**Read**
- Built-in manga reader with MangaPill, MangaKatana, MangaDex, and WeebCentral
- Webtoon scroll, left-to-right, or right-to-left reading modes
- Reading color filters: Sepia, Invert, Night Warmth, Monochrome

**Stay up to date**
- Weekly airing schedule in your local timezone, with live countdowns
- Anime news from Crunchyroll, ANN, MyAnimeList, and Anime Corner
- Anime openings and endings with a background mini player

**Sync**
- One-tap AniList login with two-way progress, scores, and list sync
- Custom lists, plus Planning, Watching, Completed, Paused, and Dropped

**Customize (MySpace)**
- Color palettes: Teal, Rose Quartz, Cyberpunk Neon, Sunset Orange, Deep Violet, and more
- Fonts: JetBrains Mono, Inter, Poppins, Outfit
- Avatars, banners, and profile backgrounds

## Installation

1. Go to the **[latest release](https://github.com/Lovelakshya1/himmy-releases/releases/latest)**.
2. Download **`app-release.apk`**.
3. Open it on your Android device.
4. If asked, allow installs from unknown sources.
5. Tap **Install** and launch the app.

> Requires Android 7.0 (API 24) or higher.

## Updating

- **In the app (recommended):** MySpace → Check Updates → Download and Install.
- **From GitHub:** download the newest APK from [Releases](https://github.com/Lovelakshya1/himmy-releases/releases) and install it over your current version. Your history, bookmarks, and settings are kept.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it's needed |
| :--- | :--- |
| `INTERNET` / `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Streaming, manga pages, metadata, and AniList sync |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` / `DATA_SYNC` | Keeping downloads running in the background |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Download progress and playback controls |
| `REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES` | In-app updates |
